# 📊 Sales Performance & Business Analytics Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed using **Power BI** to help management monitor business performance, target achievement, profitability, operational risks, and customer experience.

The dashboard is designed to support monthly business reviews and enable data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations and KPI monitoring.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

A regional sales company operates across multiple regions and product categories. Leadership needed a centralized dashboard to answer critical business questions:

✅ Is the business growing year-over-year?

✅ Are sales targets being achieved?

✅ Which Regional Managers are performing best?

✅ Which categories are underperforming?

✅ Are product returns increasing?

✅ Are delivery delays affecting customer experience?

The objective was to transform fragmented business data into a meaningful decision-support system.
