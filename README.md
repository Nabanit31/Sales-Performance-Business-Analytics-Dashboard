# Sales Performance & Business Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This project is an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed using Power BI to help management monitor business performance, target achievement, profitability, operational risks, and customer experience.

The dashboard was designed to support monthly business reviews and provide actionable insights for leadership through interactive visualizations and KPI monitoring.

---

## Business Problem

A regional sales company operates across multiple regions and product categories. Leadership wanted a centralized dashboard to answer key business questions:

* Is the business growing year-over-year?
* Are sales targets being achieved?
* Which Regional Managers are performing well?
* Which categories are underperforming?
* Are product returns increasing?
* Are delivery delays affecting customer experience?

The objective was to transform fragmented business data into a meaningful decision-making tool.

---

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of business performance through:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Sales YoY %
* Profit YoY %
* Achievement %
* Return %
* Delay %

Includes sales trends, category performance, and regional analysis.

---

### 2. Target Achievement Dashboard

Analyzes performance against assigned sales targets.

Features:

* Sales vs Target
* Achievement %
* Sales Gap
* Regional Manager Performance
* Category-wise Achievement
* Manager-Category Performance Matrix

Helps identify target shortfalls and high-performing managers.

---

### 3. Risk & Operational Performance Dashboard

Focuses on operational efficiency and customer experience.

Features:

* Returned Orders
* Return Rate
* Delayed Orders
* Delay Rate
* Returns by Category
* Returns by Region
* Delays by Region
* Delays by Shipping Mode

Highlights operational risks affecting business performance.

---

### 4. Business Summary & Future Roadmap

Summarizes:

* Key Business Insights
* Risks & Challenges
* Recommended Actions
* Future Growth Opportunities
* Analytics Roadmap

## Key Insights

* Business exceeded overall sales targets.
* Technology emerged as the top-performing category.
* Strong year-over-year growth in both sales and profit.
* Return rates remained relatively low.
* Delivery delays were identified as the primary operational risk.
* Regional and category-level performance gaps were clearly identified.
